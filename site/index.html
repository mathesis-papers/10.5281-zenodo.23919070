MathOverflow 516405, revision 1: the second adjacency eigenvalue of Apollonian networks and the fixed-rank extreme spectrum

Published paper · Version 2 · CC BY 4.0

Abstract

We prove the affirmative answer to MathOverflow question 516405, revision 1: the second ordinary adjacency eigenvalue of deterministic inner-face Apollonian networks divided by 2^(m/2) tends to sqrt(2). For every fixed rank r, the largest and smallest adjacency eigenvalues divided by 2^(m/2) converge to plus and minus sqrt(d_r), where the decreasing weight sequence consists of 3, three copies of 2, and 3^j copies of 3*2^(-j) for each j >= 1. An exact cutoff Gram identity gives a finite-stage bound and an O_r(2^(-m/4)) rate. The original second-eigenvalue limit is the scope of the pinned Lean declaration; the fixed-rank and quantitative extensions have written proofs.
